  slot14 = 0x00000001, ok=1 (N4265/66) -- the next word is the constant 1 (a small flag/argument),
    so the faulting frame's slot layout is "ret_addr, 1, 0" at sp+0x10.
  slot18 = 0x00000000, ok=1 (N4267/68) -- the next word is zero (a NULL slot; a NULL here at sp+0x18 is

  ptr_word = 0x00000000, ok=0 (N4269/70) -- the read AT far (0x10&~3) was GUARDED and refused
    (vm_map_lookup_entry on far&~3 failed), so this is "unmapped", not "a word that is zero";
    combined with N4198 far_in_map=0 this is a positive confirmation that 0x10 is unmapped.
  lr_slot = 0xf0084640, ok=1 (N4271/72) -- the word at (lr-4)&~3 = 0x381959ac is 0xf0084640.
    BY VALUE this word does NOT encode a `bl` to pc. A Thumb-2 BL target is Align(PC,4) + imm,
    imm = S:i1:i2:imm10:imm11 with i1 = NOT(J1 EOR S), i2 = NOT(J2 EOR S). An exhaustive search of
    all 2^25 imm values shows the ONLY imms that land Align(PC,4) on 0x381959ac from pc are (as

    halfword at the slot would be 0xF007 with imm11 = 0x2c4..0x2cb, and the captured halfword is
    0xf008 with imm11 = 0x464 -- not in that (or any other) window. pc's low bits are absorbed by
    Align(PC,4), so the search covers every convention. So "lr_slot decodes as a real
    call-return" (records/1033-reading.txt:20) is a CHECKER
    VERDICT (lr_slot_ok=1 means only "the copyin succeeded", patch_d13_eval_frame.py:107), not a value.
