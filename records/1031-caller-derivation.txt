*** DECISIVE CONSEQUENCE ***
The caller does NOT compute the region's exclusive top. It builds r3 (the writer's r4) as the LOW end of a
`count`-word VLA buffer carved BELOW sp (block2_lo = sp0 - 8*count), and it is the WRITER's post-increment
cursor that walks UP. So the 1032 design's central premise - that 1031's cell will name a caller-side
computation of the TOP (CAL-CALLER-TOP) or of a base+size (CAL-CALLER-SIZE) - is NOT what the instructions
show: the caller's r3 is a plain below-sp VLA base, neither a top nor a base+size-of-the-region. The cell
1031 prints will compare r3 (=a VLA base near sp) against region_end; it should read r3 << region_end, i.e.
NEITHER CAL-CALLER-TOP NOR CAL-CALLER-SIZE, i.e. **CAL-CALLER-OTHER** unless the block2 buffer genuinely
reaches 0x2bdae000.
The open quantity is still `count` (r5, the return of 0x37c26698): it sets how far block2 reaches. If
count is large enough that block2_lo + 4*count (== block1_lo) reaches the region top, the OVERFLOW is in
this caller's own VLA sizing; if not, the writer's cursor runs PAST its own buffer. Either way the fix
belongs in THIS caller (its block sizing / count), NOT in any region geometry - consistent with s0.2.
