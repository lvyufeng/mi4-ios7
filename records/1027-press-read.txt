WHAT THIS DECIDES.  The region [0x2bce1000, 0x2bde0000) has its EXCLUSIVE TOP at far, and the word
just below far (far-4) is mapped.  A write AT far is a write ONE WORD PAST the region's end.  H1
(the walk using a different map than create_unix_stack allocated into) is REFUTED by value: map ==
taskmap.  So the cause is upstream of vm_fault: the initial argv/env block / sp is placed so that its
top EQUALS the region top, and userspace's first touch of the word at the top faults.

THE FIX (docs/1027-design.md section 7, chosen against THIS number): kernel-side, give the initial
block / sp one word (16 B) of headroom so the block top is STRICTLY below the region top.  The layout
comment in exec_copyout_strings already DESCRIBES a "16b" reserved slot at p->user_stack that the
placement does not HONOUR; the fix is to honour it in create_unix_stack (reserve the top so the block
top < far), not to move sp's alignment or touch MAXSSIZ / LAB_USER_STACK / VM_MAX_ADDRESS.

BY-PRODUCT (a real defect, fixed): the resident-rung press scripts (1024/1025/1026/1027) killed the
background USB reader then ran an UNGUARDED `wait "$USB_PID"`; a SIGTERM'd reader makes wait return
143 and `set -e` ABORTED THE SCRIPT before the USB verdict, the recovery, and the reading.  That is
why the 1027 press's live log ended at the runner's line with no capture verdict -- the capture above
came from the FLASHED ROM after the operator's power press, not from the USB stream.  Fixed with
`set +e` around the wait ([[mi4-unguarded-wait-aborts-resident-press]]).
