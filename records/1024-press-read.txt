THE READING
  THE PAYLOAD NEVER ENUMERATED.  Our device descriptor is const, so the ladder could not
  have advertised 0910 and been seen as d00d; and nothing re-enumerated at all after
  09:45:42.  The phone went dark at the bootloader->payload handoff, exactly like every
  prior non-returning rung (521, 522, 526, 662, 1023).  `xnu_live_usb_*` keys x0 is
  consistent: the ladder never reached the idle path that publishes them.

  Consequence: this press is a NON-RETURN, and it carries the SAME blind spot every
  non-return has carried - the reading is lost with the cold boot because the carrier is
  the flashed ROM.  The one lever that would have netted it, the USB RAM-console stream,
  is itself the thing the payload never armed.  A non-returning arm whose only output is
  a USB stream cannot be read on its first non-returning press; that is a property of the
  arm design, written here so the next non-returning arm budgets for it.
